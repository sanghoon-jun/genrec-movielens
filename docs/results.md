## Notes
- Backbone choice is ours: the paper uses an internal ~1B–10B LLM with Netflix-specific pretraining (Phase 1); no equivalent here
- Train time includes periodic valid eval, excludes final test eval
- Cost = pod time (training + eval) at $0.74/h (RunPod Secure RTX 4090)
- SASRec run-to-run variation on MPS (same seed): NDCG@10 0.1681 / 0.1701
- Same-settings comparisons (test NDCG@10): 0.5B v2 → v3: 0.1441 → 0.1500 (+4%); 0.5B → 1.5B (v2, lr 1e-4): 0.1386 → 0.1486 (+7%)
- Warmup + grad clip are required: without them training can collapse to popularity-level
- Timestamps are noisy: 52.9% of consecutive ratings share a timestamp → low ceiling for next-item metrics
